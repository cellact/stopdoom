Privacy

What stays on the phone

StopDoom is made by Cellact BV. The app has no account and no StopDoom server. The limits, the open count, Work mode, and the streak stay on the device.

This policy was last updated on 9 November 2026.

iPhone

StopDoom uses Apple Screen Time, called Family Controls. It is the only way Apple lets an app lock other apps. You choose the apps in Apple’s own picker. StopDoom keeps that choice on the phone as a private token. Apple does not give StopDoom the messages, photos, or screen inside those apps. When you open a locked app, iOS shows StopDoom’s lock screen and counts that open.

Android

  • Usage access. While a lock is on, StopDoom asks Android which app just came to the front, so it can count an open and show the lock. It does not read the screen, what you type, notifications, photos, or messages.
  • Display over other apps. This is the lock screen. It does not record what is underneath.
  • Pop-up windows in the background. Only on Xiaomi, Redmi, and POCO. Their system otherwise drops the lock screen.
  • Battery. So Android does not stop the lock. Android then shows a small ongoing notification, “StopDoom is on”. It has no personal data.
  • App list. Names and icons of apps with a home-screen icon, so you can pick what to lock. The package names of the ones you lock stay on the phone.

What StopDoom never does

  • Read messages, photos, passwords, or what you type.
  • See what you watch or browse inside another app.
  • Send any of this to a server, an advertiser, or another person.
  • Ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • Watch someone else’s phone. The lock is for you, on your phone.
  • Sell data, or use it for ads.

If you delete StopDoom

Your limits and today’s count are deleted with the app. The system switches stay on until you turn them off in the phone’s Settings. After StopDoom is gone, it cannot use them.
