Stop the doomscroll.

StopDoom locks the apps that pull you in. You choose how many times a day they may open, and for how long. When the opens are gone, go to work.

What it does

  • You pick the apps. StopDoom does not pick them for you.
  • Each one gets a few opens a day. Work mode locks all of them until a timer ends.
  • A streak counts the days the locks stay on, and the time you did not spend.
  • English, Spanish, Dutch, German, and Hebrew.

On your phone

On iPhone, StopDoom uses Apple Screen Time. On Android, it uses usage access and a lock screen over the app you opened. The choices stay on the phone. There is no account.
