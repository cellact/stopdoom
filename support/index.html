Help with StopDoom

There is no account to sign in to. Everything you set lives on the phone.

The lock does not appear

  • On iPhone, open StopDoom and allow Screen Time. Apple asks once.
  • On Android, allow usage access and display over other apps. On Xiaomi, Redmi, and POCO, also allow pop-up windows in the background, or the lock screen is thrown away.
  • If Android stops the lock to save battery, allow StopDoom to ignore battery saving.

Language

Choose English, Spanish, Dutch, German, or Hebrew during setup, or later in Settings. Hebrew lays the app out from the right. The app reloads once so the direction can change.

Pause or reset

A pause is in Settings, under Advanced. It waits 30 seconds, then asks you to type a sentence. Reset is in the same place. It unlocks every app and deletes your groups, Work mode, and streak.
